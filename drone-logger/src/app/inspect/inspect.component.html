Inspector Beta

Load slow footage of power lines, solar panels, fences, gates or buildings. Each sampled frame is checked for them, and anything that looks worth a closer look is flagged. It all runs on this computer: the video isn't uploaded.

Beta: the Inspector is new and still learning. It can miss things and flag things that are fine, so treat every finding as a hint to look closer, check it yourself, and don't rely on it as your only check of power lines or other live equipment.
